Day by Day
Arrival — Sat, Oct 3: Land in Rome ▼
11:50am · FCO

Land at Fiumicino — American Airlines from Nashville via Charlotte. Through passport control and bags, then a private transfer or taxi to Parco dei Principi in Parioli, ~45 min on the north side of the city.

Afternoon

Check in and don’t go far. Villa Borghese is right outside the hotel — the perfect soft landing. Stroll the park, let Levi run, ride the little boats on the lake, gelato. The hotel’s garden pool is there if anyone needs to crash.

Evening

Early, easy dinner in Parioli near the hotel — don’t fight across the city tonight. Everyone makes it to dinner, then lights out. Tomorrow Rome begins for real.

Day 1 — Sun, Oct 4: Jet Lag Recovery ▼
Morning

No alarm. Breakfast at the hotel, then walk straight into Villa Borghese next door — the gardens are your front yard here. Let Levi run, let Logan stare at the umbrella pines, let the grandmas decompress.

Midday

Easy lunch in Parioli — no plans, no reservations. A neighborhood trattoria or pizza al taglio. Grab water, snacks, and diapers at a Conad or Carrefour Express near the hotel.

Afternoon · 2–5pm

Nap rotation. Someone sleeps while someone else takes the kids back into the park or down to the hotel’s garden pool. The Bioparco (Rome’s zoo) is also right inside Villa Borghese if Levi needs a win.

Evening · 7pm

Keep it local — an easy dinner in Parioli or up at lively Ponte Milvio, both minutes from the hotel. Save Trastevere (a 15–20 min taxi south) for a night when everyone’s rested. Zero pressure today.

Day 2 — Mon, Oct 5: Colosseum + The Rome You Haven't Seen ▼
Morning · 9am

Colosseum + Roman Forum. Skip-the-line tickets. Logan in carrier, Levi goes gladiator-mode. Quick Forum loop — Arch of Titus, Temple of Saturn, Senate House. New: book the Underground + Arena Floor tour — Levi walks where gladiators actually stood.

Midday · 12:30pm

Lunch in Monti. Ai Tre Scalini on Via Panisperna — classic trattoria, outdoor seating, kid-friendly. Alt: La Carbonara at Campo de' Fiori.

Afternoon · 2–4pm

Back for Logan's nap + everyone's reset. Non-negotiable day-one energy management.

Late Afternoon · 5pm

Aventine Hill — the Rome most tourists skip. Start at Giardino degli Aranci (Orange Garden) for the best sunset panorama of the city — St. Peter's framed by umbrella pines. Then walk 2 min to the Knights of Malta Keyhole — peek through the keyhole in the green door and see St. Peter's dome perfectly framed by a hedge tunnel. Levi will lose his mind. Gelato at Fatamorgana on the walk back.

Evening · 7:30pm

Trastevere. Da Enzo al 29 — cacio e pepe, carciofi alla giudia. No reservations, arrive 7pm. Backup: Tonnarello — outdoor tables, huge portions, kid-friendly.

Day 3 — Tue, Oct 6: Testaccio, Borghese & Rooftop Aperitivo ▼
Morning · 9:30am

Testaccio Market — Rome's real food neighborhood, zero tourists. Sample supplì (fried rice balls), trapizzino (pizza pockets stuffed with Roman stews), fresh pasta, seasonal produce. Levi can point and eat. Walk past the Pyramid of Cestius — yes, Rome has a legit Egyptian pyramid. Levi content.

Midday · 12pm

Lunch at Flavio al Velavevodetto — built into ancient Monte Testaccio (a hill of Roman pottery shards). Their carbonara and amatriciana are top-three-in-the-city good. Or Trapizzino for something faster with kids.

Afternoon · 2pm

Villa Borghese — right beside your hotel, so this one’s an easy stroll. Family rickshaw bike, lake, Pincio Terrace panorama. Highly recommend: Borghese Gallery (book months ahead) — small, intimate, no crowds. Grandmas take kids in park while you and Lindsey see Bernini's sculptures up close. 2-hour timed entry keeps it civilized.

Late Afternoon

Explore Via Margutta — the artists' street where Audrey Hepburn lived in Roman Holiday. Tiny galleries, ivy-covered walls, quiet. Then stroll through Piazza del Popolo and duck into Santa Maria del Popolo to see two Caravaggios for free in a side chapel — better than fighting museum crowds.

Evening · 7pm

Aperitivo at Terrazza Borromini rooftop overlooking Piazza Navona — or Hotel Raphael rooftop same area. Dinner at Roscioli — reserve ahead. The carbonara is perfect.

Day 4 — Wed, Oct 7: Rome → Pisa → Leaning Tower → Lucca ▼
Morning · 9am

Pantheon (free, 20 min). Piazza Navona. Coffee at Sant’Eustachio. Jewish Ghetto — carciofi at Nonna Betta.

Midday · 12pm

Train from Roma Termini to Pisa Centrale via Trenitalia — direct Frecciarossa, ~3 hours, book Prima class for flat seats with Logan. Arrive Pisa mid-afternoon.

Afternoon · 3pm

Pick up rental car at Pisa station (or Galilei airport branch, 5 min taxi). Drive 10 min to Piazza dei Miracoli — Leaning Tower, Cathedral, Baptistery. 30–45 min to walk the piazza and get photos. Parking: Via Pietrasantina lots (north side, cheaper, 5 min walk). You don’t need to climb the Tower — the piazza is the show.

Late Afternoon · 4:30pm

Drive Pisa to Lucca — 25 min straight north on the A11. Check into Casa Maria. Groceries at Esselunga or Conad. Unpack, settle the kids, open a bottle on the patio.

Evening · 7:30pm

Easy first dinner at the villa or drive into Lucca for Buca di Sant’Antonio (since 1782).

Day by Day
Day 5 — Thu, Oct 8: Explore Lucca + Rehearsal Dinner ▼
Morning · 9am

Drive into Lucca. Rent bikes on Lucca's Renaissance walls — 4km flat tree-lined path, baby seats available. Signature Lucca experience. Piazza dell'Anfiteatro, Via Fillungo, buccellato at Taddeucci.

Midday · 12:30pm

Lunch at Trattoria da Leo — bustling local favorite. Or Buca di Sant'Antonio (since 1782). Tordelli lucchesi, zuppa di farro.

Afternoon · 3pm

Back to villa. Rest and get ready. Dress code: Garden Attire (sundresses, floral prints, lighter-hue suits).

Kids Logistics

Children not invited. Cindy + Lynda have kids at villa. Stock up: dinner for grandmas + kids, bottles/food for Logan, entertainment for Levi. Villa is walking distance from venue.

Evening · 6–10pm

Rehearsal dinner pizza party at Casale de' Pasquinelli.

Day 6 — Fri, Oct 9: The Wedding ▼
Morning

Long morning at villa — breakfast, pool, family time. Ceremony at 3:30pm.

Afternoon · 2:30pm

Get ready. Dress code: Semi-Formal (suits/cocktail, tuxes/gowns welcome). Feed Logan. Instructions for grandmas. Make sure they have car keys.

3:30pm – 1:00am

Wedding ceremony + reception. Venue is minutes from villa. Grandma plan: dinner at villa, kids to bed at normal time, Levi watches a movie.

Day 7 — Sat, Oct 10: Pool Brunch + Wedding Recovery ▼
Morning · 10:30am–1:30pm

Pool party + brunch at venue. Dress code: Beach Attire (linen, swimsuits, sandals). Grandmas have kids.

Afternoon

Recovery mode. You were up until 1am at a Tuscan wedding — nobody needs to be in a car right now. Naps, villa time, Levi in the garden. Drive into Lucca for a slow afternoon stroll if energy permits — Torre Guinigi (230 steps, oak trees on top) is the Adam + Levi adventure.

Evening · 7:30pm

Last Lucca dinner. Buca di Sant’Antonio — Lucca’s most storied restaurant, open since 1782. Pack tonight for tomorrow’s drive south.

Day 8 — Sun, Oct 11: Tuscany Morning → The Riviera ▼
Morning

Slow start at Casa Maria, then a flat, stroller-easy Tuscany morning 10 minutes away at the Villa Reale di Marlia — royal villa gardens, fountains and lawns, gravel paths the stroller handles. Levi runs, the grandmas swoon. (Open daily 10am through Oct 29 — buy tickets online.)

Midday

Long lunch at a family-run winery in the Lucchese hills — Fattoria Sardi or Fattoria Colle Verde. Book ahead and mention the baby; they’ll set up shade and a spot for the stroller.

Afternoon

Drive north to Sestri Levante — 1h10 on the A12, the easiest leg of the trip. Check into Hotel Vis à Vis, perched over the bay.

Evening

Golden hour on the Baia del Silenzio — flat promenade, sandy bay. Dinner in the flat old town at Osteria Mattana: trofie al pesto, stuffed mussels.

Day by Day
Day 9 — Mon, Oct 12: Monterosso (the easy Cinque Terre) ▼
Morning

Train from Sestri to Monterosso — ~40 min, mostly direct. The only flat, stroller-friendly village in the Cinque Terre, and the one with a real sandy beach.

Midday

Spend the whole day right here — beach, roll the promenade, long lunch, gelato. No village-hopping, no stairs, no rush. (Just Monterosso, so skip the Cinque Terre Card — plain regional tickets are all you need.)

Afternoon

Train back to Sestri by late afternoon. Pool time, then dinner in the old town.

Day 10 — Tue, Oct 13: Chiavari — Easy Riviera Day ▼
Morning

Slow morning in Sestri — beach time, no agenda.

Midday

Chiavari — ~15 min down the coast by car or a short train hop. Flat arcaded old town and a flat seafront promenade; you set the pace. Park once, settle under the porticoes for a caffè and gelato, let Levi loose in a piazza or the beachfront playground. No boat, no climbing.

Alternatives

Want the upscale-harbor feel? Santa Margherita Ligure is a ~30-min drive — same flat palm-lined promenade, still boat-free. Want nothing at all? Stay in Sestri: the Vis à Vis pool and the two bays. After a wedding, the rest day is a legit win.

Day 11 — Wed, Oct 14: Sestri → Pisa → Fly Home ▼
Morning

Last Italian breakfast over the bay. Pack slowly, one more flat stroll along the Baia del Silenzio.

Late Morning

Drive Sestri → Pisa airport (PSA), ~1h30. Drop the rental car. If the flight’s late, a flat final stop at Pisa’s Field of Miracles on the way.

Fly Home
