Day by Day
Day 1 — Sun, Oct 4: Jet Lag Recovery ▼
Morning

Sleep in. No alarm. You just crossed the Atlantic with a 6-month-old and a 4-year-old — you've earned this. When you surface, walk to the nearest bar for cornetti and cappuccini. Let Levi run. Let Logan stare at pigeons. Let the grandmas decompress.

Midday

Groceries and supplies. Hit a Conad or Carrefour Express for water, snacks, diapers, wine. Stock the apartment kitchen. Lunch wherever looks good — no plans, no reservations, no agenda. A pizza al taglio spot or any trattoria with outdoor seating.

Afternoon · 2–5pm

Nap rotation. Someone sleeps while someone else takes the kids for a slow walk. The neighborhood is the activity today. Find the nearest gelateria, the best coffee bar, the piazza where Levi can run in circles.

Evening · 7pm

Easy first dinner. Tonnarello in Trastevere — outdoor tables, massive portions, kid-friendly, no reservations needed. Or order in and eat at the apartment. Zero pressure. Tomorrow is when Rome begins.

Day 2 — Mon, Oct 5: Colosseum + The Rome You Haven't Seen ▼
Morning · 9am

Colosseum + Roman Forum. Skip-the-line tickets. Logan in carrier, Levi goes gladiator-mode. Quick Forum loop — Arch of Titus, Temple of Saturn, Senate House. New: book the Underground + Arena Floor tour — Levi walks where gladiators actually stood.

Midday · 12:30pm

Lunch in Monti. Ai Tre Scalini on Via Panisperna — classic trattoria, outdoor seating, kid-friendly. Alt: La Carbonara at Campo de' Fiori.

Afternoon · 2–4pm

Back for Logan's nap + everyone's reset. Non-negotiable day-one energy management.

Late Afternoon · 5pm

Aventine Hill — the Rome most tourists skip. Start at Giardino degli Aranci (Orange Garden) for the best sunset panorama of the city — St. Peter's framed by umbrella pines. Then walk 2 min to the Knights of Malta Keyhole — peek through the keyhole in the green door and see St. Peter's dome perfectly framed by a hedge tunnel. Levi will lose his mind. Gelato at Fatamorgana on the walk back.

Evening · 7:30pm

Trastevere. Da Enzo al 29 — cacio e pepe, carciofi alla giudia. No reservations, arrive 7pm. Backup: Tonnarello — outdoor tables, huge portions, kid-friendly.

Day 3 — Tue, Oct 6: Testaccio, Borghese & Rooftop Aperitivo ▼
Morning · 9:30am

Testaccio Market — Rome's real food neighborhood, zero tourists. Sample supplì (fried rice balls), trapizzino (pizza pockets stuffed with Roman stews), fresh pasta, seasonal produce. Levi can point and eat. Walk past the Pyramid of Cestius — yes, Rome has a legit Egyptian pyramid. Levi content.

Midday · 12pm

Lunch at Flavio al Velavevodetto — built into ancient Monte Testaccio (a hill of Roman pottery shards). Their carbonara and amatriciana are top-three-in-the-city good. Or Trapizzino for something faster with kids.

Afternoon · 2pm

Villa Borghese park. Family rickshaw bike, lake, Pincio Terrace panorama. Highly recommend: Borghese Gallery (book months ahead) — small, intimate, no crowds. Grandmas take kids in park while you and Lindsey see Bernini's sculptures up close. 2-hour timed entry keeps it civilized.

Late Afternoon

Explore Via Margutta — the artists' street where Audrey Hepburn lived in Roman Holiday. Tiny galleries, ivy-covered walls, quiet. Then stroll through Piazza del Popolo and duck into Santa Maria del Popolo to see two Caravaggios for free in a side chapel — better than fighting museum crowds.

Evening · 7pm

Aperitivo at Terrazza Borromini rooftop overlooking Piazza Navona — or Hotel Raphael rooftop same area. Dinner at Roscioli — reserve ahead. The carbonara is perfect.

Day 4 — Wed, Oct 7: Morning Rome → Train to Lucca ▼
Morning · 9am

Pantheon (free, 20 min). Piazza Navona. Coffee at Sant'Eustachio. Jewish Ghetto — carciofi at Nonna Betta.

Afternoon · 1pm

Train to Lucca via Trenitalia (Frecciarossa Prima class to Florence, regional to Lucca). Or private car service (~€400–500, zero logistics with baby).

Evening · 5pm

Arrive Lucca. Pick up rental car. Check into villa. Groceries at Esselunga/Conad. Easy dinner at villa or Buca di Sant'Antonio.

3:30pm – 1:00am

Day 7 — Sat, Oct 10: Pool Brunch + Wedding Recovery ▼

Pool party + brunch at venue. Dress code: Beach Attire (linen, swimsuits, sandals). Grandmas have kids.

Afternoon

Recovery mode. You were up until 1am at a Tuscan wedding — nobody needs to be in a car right now. Naps, villa time, Levi in the garden. Drive into Lucca for a slow afternoon stroll if energy permits — Torre Guinigi (230 steps, oak trees on top) is the Adam + Levi adventure.

Evening · 7:30pm

Dinner at Gli Orti di Via Elisa — garden setting, perfect October evening. One more full day in Lucca tomorrow before heading south.

Day 8 — Sun, Oct 11: Bonus Lucca Day ▼
Morning

Sleep in. Villa breakfast on the patio. No rush — Castello Di Viona isn’t available until tomorrow.

Late Morning · 11am

Drive into Lucca for the things you missed. Torre Guinigi if you haven’t done it yet (230 steps, oak trees on top). Browse the shops on Via Fillungo. Pick up olive oil and wine to bring home.

Afternoon

Villa time. Pool (if open), garden, naps. Or explore nearby hill towns — Montecarlo (15 min drive) has a medieval fortress and wine tastings.

Evening · 7:30pm

Last Lucca dinner. Buca di Sant’Antonio — Lucca’s most storied restaurant, open since 1782. Pack tonight for tomorrow’s drive south.

Last coffee at Casa Maria. Load the car. Say goodbye to the Lucchese hills.

Late Morning · 10am

Drive to Val d’Orcia (~2–2.5 hrs). Route through Siena — gelato in the Campo if timing works. Lunch in Siena or push through to Castello Di Viona.

Afternoon

Check into Castello Di Viona. Settle in, explore the property. Let the landscape hit you.

Evening

Dinner on-site or nearby trattoria. First Brunello of the leg.

Day by Day
Day 10 — Tue, Oct 13: The Tuscan Drive ▼
Stop 1 · Pienza

Pienza — the pecorino capital. Buy cheese everywhere. The view from the town walls = every Tuscany calendar. Family photo here.

Stop 2 · Montepulciano

Montepulciano — wine tasting at Cantina Contucci (making wine since 1008). Lunch at Osteria Acquacheta — famous bistecca, shared tables. Reserve ahead.

Afternoon Drive

Back roads between Montepulciano and Montalcino. Famous cypress road near Cappella della Madonna di Vitaleta. Stop whenever the light does something.

Stop 3 · Montalcino

Fortezza di Montalcino wine bar — world's best Brunellos, €10–15/glass.

Evening · Date Night

Grandmas take kids. Adam + Lindsey splurge at Ristorante di Poggio Antico — Michelin-recognized, wine estate views.

Last Italian breakfast. Pack slowly. One more walk. Take the photos you forgot.

Late Morning

Drive to Florence airport (FLR) ~1.5–2 hrs or Pisa (PSA) ~2–2.5 hrs. Drop rental car. Fly home.
