Rome (Jet Lag & History)
Days 1–3

Arrival & Recovery

Oct 1–3

Land in Rome, settle into hotel, rest. Day 3: first neighborhood walk (Trastevere), light dinner. Keep it simple while adjusting to 9-hour time difference.

Days 4–6

Ancient Rome & Vatican

Oct 4–6

Colosseum, Roman Forum, Pantheon. Vatican Museum & Sistine Chapel (early entry). Quiet evening in the Eternal City.

Days 7–9

Local Rome & Surroundings

Oct 7–9

Spanish Steps, Trevi Fountain, Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore. Day trip to Villa d'Este in Tivoli (waterfalls, gardens). Gelato runs are non-negotiable.

Hotels

Accommodation

9 Nights

W Rome, InterContinental, Singer Palace, Six Senses, Palazzo Manfredi — rotating through premium properties in Centro Storico and Spanish Steps neighborhood.

🚴
Lucca (Medieval Walls & Wine)
Days 10–12

Arrival in Lucca

Oct 10–12

Rent car in Rome, 3.5-hour drive to Lucca. Settle into VRBO apartment. Evening walk on the medieval walls. Dinner in Piazza Anfiteatro.

Days 13–15

Bikes, Walls & Food

Oct 13–15

Bike the Renaissance walls (full loop, 4.2km). Visit San Michele in Foro and Duomo di San Martino. Wine tasting in Montecarlo. Casual cooking class.

Days 16–18

Day Trips & Villages

Oct 16–18

Pisa (Leaning Tower, Duomo). Cinque Terre day trip (hiking, pesto, swimming). Barga (hill village, views). Slow pace, local restaurants.

Base

Accommodation

9 Nights

VRBO apartment in Lucca Centro with kitchen, courtyard, period details. Perfect for cooking breakfast, family-style living.

🌻
Val d'Orcia (Tuscan Soul)
Days 19–21

Drive South

Oct 19–21

Drive from Lucca to Val d'Orcia (2.5 hours). Settle in. Evening walk through Pienza. Dinner at a family-run trattoria. Sunrise photos at the cypress trees.

Days 22–24

Countryside & Wine

Oct 22–24

Montalcino wine tasting (Brunello). Montepulciano (Vino Nobile, medieval streets). Cooking class with local chef (fresh pasta, truffle hunt). Late-afternoon drives through golden hills.

Days 25–27

Villages & Rest

Oct 25–27

San Quirico d'Orcia, Radicofani (hilltop fortress). Farmers market. Slow mornings, afternoon siestas. Outdoor dinners as weather permits. Family time.

Base

Accommodation

9 Nights

Agriturismo or villa rental with pool, views, and traditional Tuscan architecture. Feels like home in the countryside.

Trip Logistics
Flights
BNA → FCO (Sept 30–Oct 1) + Rome→BNA (Oct 27–28). Return via plane, not car in Tuscany.
Cars
Pick up Audi Q7 or VW Multivan in Rome for Lucca drive. Keep entire trip (leave in Val d'Orcia or return to Rome before flights).
Languages
Italian basics. Book restaurants and tours in advance. Guides for wine tastings and cooking classes.
Budget
Premium hotels, VRBO, agriturismo. Michelin-starred restaurants mixed with local spots. Plan for ~€10-12K total.
Kids
Logan (6 months) & Felix (2.5 yrs). Daytime naps, early dinners, family-friendly activities. No heavy touring on fussy days.
Visas & Docs
US passport. No visa needed for Italy (EU). Bring birth certificates for babies, travel permits if needed.
